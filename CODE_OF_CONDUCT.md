# Code of Conduct

GolangForAll welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels,
identities, and perspectives. We want participation in our issues, pull
requests, documentation, and community spaces to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers find context.
- Respect privacy and give credit for others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, unwanted
sexual attention, and repeated disruptive or abusive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/DhawalShankar). Do not post private information in
a public issue. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
needed to keep the community safe and constructive.
